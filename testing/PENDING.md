**How the picture looks**

- **K13. A soft darker column on the rocket's night sky.** With the Rocket sample or `04-dark-rocket.jpg`, **Cast shadows** on and the light at the side, the sky to the right of each tower is a little darker, as a blurred column. The one on the right can have a fairly definite top edge. Not changed this round. Cases: S-07, T-04, Q-06.
- **K14. The cup's shadow on the saucer is fainter than it should be.** The app cannot tell "saucer just behind the cup" from "sky far behind a tower", so both get the soft, faint kind of shadow. The cup still shades the saucer and the table. Not changed this round. Cases: S-01, S-06, Q-02.
- **K15. A faint dark fringe beside a far shadow.** The wide blur of a far shadow can spill a few pixels onto whatever is next to it, including the object that casts it. Not changed this round. Cases: S-06, Q-04.
- **K17. A thin light outline with Detailed normals off.** With the **Detailed normals** switch off, a thin light line shows round the astronaut's head and shoulders. Cases: P-04, Q-08.
- **K18. Intensity 0.00 is not black.** With **Intensity** at 0.00 the picture is very dark but still visible, because the **Ambient** light (0.06) still reaches it. Set Ambient to 0.00 as well for black. Case: C-02.
- **K11. Stand-in Enhance: the two halves are nearly the same.** In the normal setup Enhance is a stand-in, so "Enhanced" looks like "Live preview"; tiny differences in shadow softness are expected (two different programs draw them). Cases: E-03, Q-10.
- **K19. Plan B (mock mode): an uploaded photo is lit as a round dome.** In mock mode an uploaded file gets a made-up shape, and the edge of the dome shows as a circle. The four samples are lit correctly in mock mode. Case: R-08.

**Buttons, words and layout**

- **K27. On a phone 320 pixels wide, "Reset light" drops to a second line.** From 360 pixels wide the five preset buttons share one line. At 320 the last one sits alone on a second line, as wide as the panel. Case: W-17.
- **K28. On a phone, the link in the demo's card breaks in the middle of a word.** "github.com/aarooshpravici/BanannaHacksSubm" / "ission". Only on the static demo. Case: H-05.
- **K29. The "Live shading is unavailable" bar cannot be closed.** It has no X. With the backend it may have a **Retry** link; on the demo it has neither. It stays above the stage for as long as the photo is open. Cases: R-06, R-11, R-20, H-07.
- **K30. A failed Enhance makes the photo a little smaller.** On a window 1024 pixels wide or more the red bar sits above the photo and takes its height from the stage (two lines of it at 1280 pixels wide). The photo grows back when you close the bar. Cases: R-03, R-17, R-21.
- **K31. Two different messages for a failed Enhance with the backend stopped.** On an uploaded photo: "Cannot reach the backend at …. Is it running?". On a sample: "Enhance needs the backend, which cannot be reached at …. The live preview and Export PNG still work without it." Cases: R-03, R-17.
- **K32. No live shading, and the backend stops after a picture arrived: the old picture stays.** The last picture from the backend stays on screen, and **Hold to see original** and **Export PNG** stay usable, while the readout already shows the new light. Export then saves a picture that does not match the readout. Case: R-11.
- **K33. The first arrow press after a preset can be a jump.** If a preset has put the light beyond the edge of the stage (for example **Top** on the astronaut), the first arrow press back towards the photo moves the light to the stage's edge in one jump, and the sun does not visibly move. Later presses move one step each. Cases: L-12, W-10.
- **K34. No live shading: a Sweep that is already running does not stop when the backend fails.** The controls grey out, but the sweep runs to its end (at most 6 seconds). Case: R-11.
- **K35. A missing full stop in one red bar.** Without live shading, after the backend was restarted, the bar can read "… failed: unknown scene id; upload the photo again The light controls are off until a render arrives." Case: R-11.
- **K36. A static demo build only works at the address it was built for.** A demo built for the folder `/BanannaHacksSubmission` does not work when served from the top of a site (no pictures, and nothing reacts), and the other way round. The command that builds it is written for macOS and Linux and is expected to fail on Windows. Cases: H-06, H-08.
