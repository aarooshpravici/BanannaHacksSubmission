**How the picture looks**

- **K13. A soft darker column on the rocket's night sky.** With the Rocket sample or `04-dark-rocket.jpg`, **Cast shadows** on and the light at the side, the sky to the right of each tower is a little darker, as a blurred column. The one on the right can have a fairly definite top edge. Not changed this round. Cases: S-07, T-04, Q-06.
- **K14. The cup's shadow on the saucer is fainter than it should be.** The app cannot tell "saucer just behind the cup" from "sky far behind a tower", so both get the soft, faint kind of shadow. The cup still shades the saucer and the table. Not changed this round. Cases: S-01, S-06, Q-02.
- **K15. A faint dark fringe beside a far shadow.** The wide blur of a far shadow can spill a few pixels onto whatever is next to it, including the object that casts it. Not changed this round. Cases: S-06, Q-04.
- **K17. A thin light outline with Detailed normals off.** With the **Detailed normals** switch off, a thin light line shows round the astronaut's head and shoulders. Cases: P-04, Q-08.
- **K18. Intensity 0.00 is not black.** With **Intensity** at 0.00 the picture is very dark but still visible, because the **Ambient** light (0.06) still reaches it. Set Ambient to 0.00 as well for black. Case: C-02.
- **K11. Stand-in Enhance: the two halves are nearly the same.** In the normal setup Enhance is a stand-in, so "Enhanced" looks like "Live preview"; tiny differences in shadow softness are expected (two different programs draw them). Cases: E-03, Q-10.
- **K19. Plan B (mock mode): the round dome is now shown only if you ask for it.** Until the late night update of Oct 10, Plan B lit every uploaded photo as a made-up dome; a tester reported that as a bug (issue 3). Now the browser works out the photo's real shape. The dome is left only behind the button **Use a stand-in dome**, on the card that shows when the browser cannot analyse the photo, and the grey note "No backend is running (mock mode), so this photo is lit as a made-up dome…" still says so. A dome without having clicked that button is a bug: report it. Cases: R-08, R-23.
- **K48. Your own photo, analysed in the browser, is flatter on a face than a sample, with a thin outline.** On the demo, in Plan B and after **Analyse in this browser**, a face has less light and shade on the nose and cheeks than the Portrait sample has, and a thin light-and-dark outline shows round the hair and shoulders. The browser runs only the depth model and takes the surface directions from the slope of the depth map; the second, larger model (Marigold) runs only on the Relight server. It is the same look as K17. The GEOMETRY panel and "How it works" say so. Your opinion on how much it matters is wanted. Cases: Q-12, P-12, H-12.

**Buttons, words and layout**

- **K27. On a phone 320 pixels wide, "Reset light" drops to a second line.** From 360 pixels wide the five preset buttons share one line. At 320 the last one sits alone on a second line, as wide as the panel. Case: W-17.
- **K29. The red "Live shading is unavailable" bar cannot be closed.** It has no X. With the backend it may have a **Retry** link; on the demo it has neither. It stays above the stage for as long as the photo is open. Cases: F-04, H-11.
- **K30. A failed Enhance makes the photo a little smaller.** On a window 1024 pixels wide or more the red bar sits above the photo and takes its height from the stage (two lines of it at 1280 pixels wide). The photo grows back when you close the bar. Cases: R-03, R-17, R-21.
- **K31. Two different messages for a failed Enhance with the backend stopped.** On an uploaded photo: "Cannot reach the backend at …. Is it running?". On a sample: "Enhance needs the backend, which cannot be reached at …. The live preview and Export PNG still work without it." Cases: R-03, R-17.
- **K32. No live shading, and the backend stops after a picture arrived: the old picture stays.** The last picture from the backend stays on screen, and **Hold to see original** and **Export PNG** stay usable, while the readout already shows the new light. Export then saves a picture that does not match the readout. Only in the red-bar state. Case: F-04.
- **K33. The first arrow press after a preset can be a jump.** If a preset has put the light beyond the edge of the stage (for example **Top** on the astronaut), the first arrow press back towards the photo moves the light to the stage's edge in one jump, and the sun does not visibly move. Later presses move one step each. Cases: L-12, W-10.
- **K34. No live shading: a Sweep that is already running does not stop when the backend fails.** The controls grey out, but the sweep runs to its end (at most 6 seconds). Only in the red-bar state. Case: F-04.
- **K35. A missing full stop in one red bar.** Without live shading, after the backend was restarted, the bar can read "… failed: unknown scene id; upload the photo again The light controls are off until a render arrives." Case: F-04.
- **K36. A static demo build only works at the address it was built for.** A demo built for the folder `/BanannaHacksSubmission` does not work when served from the top of a site (no pictures, and nothing reacts), and the other way round. The command that builds it is written for macOS and Linux and is expected to fail on Windows. Cases: H-06, H-08.

**Shading paths and the "How it works" card (new in the night update)**

- **K39. When the graphics chip stops, about 2.5 seconds of plain photo with no message.** The photo shows without lighting while the sun, the sliders and the buttons look normal and do nothing. Then the grey note appears and the processor takes over. Case: R-12.
- **K40. Doubled punctuation in the red bar.** It reads "… could not create a 2D canvas.). Showing server renders instead …" or "… 2D canvas.), and the server render …". Cases: F-04, H-11.
- **K43. On the processor, the quick preview is hard to tell from the sharp picture.** On a sample photo at desktop size the preview is only slightly softer. You may not see the picture sharpen when the light stops. Case: R-11.
- **K44. The grey note cannot be closed, and it takes room.** It has no X. It is two lines long on a laptop window and four on a phone, the photo is a little smaller or lower because of it, and it stays on the Enhanced tab. Cases: R-06, R-11, H-07.
- **K45. With "shading: processor" (no "in the background"), the sun and the sliders can stutter.** That browser cannot start a background thread, so the page is busy while each picture is drawn. Case: R-11.
- **K46. With the "How it works" card open, the page behind can still scroll.** Only when the page is longer than the window (a narrow window, or a phone-sized one where the card does not fill the screen) and the mouse wheel is turned over the dimmed part. Nothing behind the card can be clicked. Case: G-03.
- **K47. On a phone the pill says only "online".** Below 640 pixels wide "online, mock providers" and the real-Enhance "online" look the same except for the dot (amber for the stand-in, green for real Enhance), and a phone shows no hover text. Case: G-06.

**Your own photo with no backend, and pictures from other web pages (new in the late night update)**

- **K49. The pill reads "offline" for up to 10 seconds after the backend is back.** The page asks the backend how it is only every 10 seconds. In that time the **Detailed normals** switch can already succeed while the pill is still red. Case: R-22.
- **K50. A picture address that does not answer: 15 seconds of "Reading image…" with no way to stop it.** Then the red bar "That image took too long to load. …" shows. Reloading the page is the only way out sooner. Case: U-25.
- **K51. Most web sites refuse a dragged picture.** The red bar "That image's site does not let other pages read it. This is a browser security rule, not a fault in the image. …" is the expected answer for most sites, not a bug. Copy the image and paste it, or save it and drop the file. Case: U-23.
- **K52. On very slow internet the first photo can fail with "The depth model stopped downloading", and Retry starts from zero.** Part of the download (about 5.5 MB) reports no progress. Since version `2b385ef` the page waits three minutes for that part; before, it gave up after 60 seconds, which was too short below roughly 0.75 megabits a second. Below roughly a quarter of a megabit a second that part still cannot arrive in time, so the message shows although the download was still going, and **Retry** begins it again. Report it if it happens on ordinary home internet. Cases: P-10, P-13.
- **K53. Opened by a number address, the depth model is downloaded again every time the page is loaded.** At an address such as `http://192.168.1.23:3000` (W-16 step 8) the browser does not let a page store the model, so the line "downloads a depth model of about 25 MB, once" is not true there, and the model always runs on the processor ("depth: this browser, processor (WebAssembly)"). At `localhost` and on a hosted demo address that starts with `https://` it is stored. Cases: P-11, W-16.
- **K54. Enhance on a photo analysed in the browser starts from the backend's own analysis.** In the normal app the photo is sent to the backend, which works out its shape again with detailed normals. The enhanced picture can therefore show a little more surface detail than the live preview beside it. Case: R-22.
- **K55. Backend: two Detailed normals requests at the same moment, right after the backend started, can break detailed normals until it is restarted.** If two requests overlap while the backend is loading the normals model for the first time (two tabs, or two very quick clicks), both fail, and every later one fails too, with a red bar that reads "The server hit an unexpected error. See the backend log." Restart the backend. Not caused by this update, but easier to hit now. Cases: R-22, P-04.
