# Pending: what the developer has NOT verified

The developer is an AI working on one Apple-chip Mac with Chrome. It cannot see a screen the way you do, has no Windows computer, no phone, and is not allowed to spend money on the paid Enhance service while testing. Everything below needs human eyes. **Most important first.**

Case IDs refer to [TEST_PLAN.md](TEST_PLAN.md). When you have checked an item, say so in your report with the item number (for example "PENDING 3: checked, see results"). An item keeps its number for good, so the numbers are names, not ranks: **the order of the rows is the order of importance**, and the newest items (26 and up) are near the top.

Last updated: 2026-10-10, late afternoon.

## Already known: no need to report

The developer already knows about these problems in the app. If you see one, do not open an issue for it. Carry on with the test case. Report it only if it is **worse** than the line says, or if you think it is bad enough to cost us with the judges (say so, with the K number: your opinion on priority is wanted).

The developer deletes a line when the problem is fixed. If a line is gone, the problem should be gone too: report it if you still see it. **Fixed in the afternoon update and deleted from this list:** K1 (jagged shadow blocks on the rocket's sky), K2 (sidebar did not fit a 1440 x 900 window), K3 (the round "N" badge), K4 (stray full stop in the no-live-shading bar), K6 (status pill lagging 10 seconds), K7 (a failed load looked like a loaded photo), K8 (wasted space on phones), K9 (the starting light was too subtle).

**How the picture looks**

- **K13. A soft darker column on the rocket's night sky.** With the Rocket sample or `04-dark-rocket.jpg`, **Cast shadows** on and the light at the side, the sky to the right of each tower is a little darker, as a blurred column. The one on the right can have a fairly definite top edge. The old hard blocks are gone. Cases: S-07, T-04, Q-06.
- **K14. The cup's shadow on the saucer is fainter than before.** The app cannot tell "saucer just behind the cup" from "sky far behind a tower", so both now get the soft, faint kind of shadow. The cup still shades the saucer and the table. Cases: S-01, S-06, Q-02.
- **K15. A faint dark fringe beside a far shadow.** The wide blur of a far shadow can spill a few pixels onto whatever is next to it, including the object that casts it. Cases: S-06, Q-04.
- **K16. A hard, straight shadow edge down the face with light from the right.** On the astronaut with the sun on the right (the **Right** preset, or mid-way through a Sweep), the shadow on the left half of the face has a fairly hard, straight diagonal edge. Other cast shadows are soft. Cases: C-05, L-04, L-09, S-06, Q-03, Q-04.
- **K17. A thin light outline with Detailed normals off.** With the **Detailed normals** switch off, a thin light line shows round the astronaut's head and shoulders. Cases: P-04, Q-08.
- **K18. Intensity 0.00 is not black.** With **Intensity** at 0.00 the picture is very dark but still visible, because the **Ambient** light (0.06) still reaches it. Set Ambient to 0.00 as well for black. Case: C-02.
- **K11. Stand-in Enhance: the two halves are nearly the same.** In the normal setup Enhance is a stand-in, so "Enhanced" looks like "Live preview"; tiny differences in shadow softness are expected (two different programs draw them). Cases: E-03, Q-10.
- **K19. Plan B (mock mode): an uploaded photo is lit as a round dome.** In mock mode an uploaded file gets a made-up shape, and the edge of the dome shows as a circle. The four samples are lit correctly in mock mode. Case: R-08.
- **K26. Plan B (mock mode): Detailed normals on a sample turns it into the dome.** In mock mode, clicking the **Detailed normals** switch on a sample replaces the sample's real shape with the stand-in dome. It stays a dome until you reload the page, or click **New photo** and click the sample again. Skip the switch on samples in Plan B. Cases: C-10, J-02.

**Buttons, words and layout**

- **K10. "Hold to see original" and "Stop sweep" do not turn yellow.** Both are meant to get a yellow outline and yellow words while active (held, or sweeping). They keep their normal look; only the label of the Sweep button changes. Cases: K-01, K-04, L-09.
- **K5. Presets give different numbers on different windows and photos.** The sun cannot leave the stage, so **Left** reads x -0.10 on the square astronaut in a 1440 x 900 window but -0.04 on the wider photos; **Right** reads 1.10, 1.05 or 1.04; **Top** reads y -0.07 on the astronaut and -0.15 on a wide photo. Cases: C-05, T-02, T-03, T-05, T-06.
- **K12. "New photo" and "Reset" keep some settings.** Both keep the ENHANCE text; "New photo" also keeps the **Cast shadows** and **Detailed normals** switches (picking a sample turns Detailed normals back on). Cases: U-07, C-07, P-06.
- **K20. The grey words in the ENHANCE box are cut off on a small laptop window.** On a window less than 771 pixels high (1280 x 720, 1366 x 768) the box is one line tall and its grey words stop at the right edge with no "…". Case: W-13.
- **K21. A red box in the ENHANCE panel makes the sidebar scroll.** At 1440 x 900 the sidebar fits exactly; an error message in the ENHANCE panel makes it longer than the window, so it scrolls. Cases: R-03, R-17.
- **K22. On a phone, Reset wraps to a second line.** The other four preset buttons fill the first line. Cases: W-04, W-15.
- **K23. On a phone, the failed-load card hides a wide photo.** The card is bigger than the dimmed photo behind it. Both buttons on the card work. Case: R-19.
- **K24. "Failed to fetch" when a sample cannot be loaded.** If the sample files cannot be fetched at all, the red bar shows the browser's own words ("Failed to fetch" in Chrome), not a sentence of ours. Case: U-22.
- **K25. No live shading, sample photo, backend stopped: the page contradicts itself.** The first red bar still says "Showing server renders instead" although none can arrive, and the sun, sliders, **Sweep** and **Enhance** stay usable although the picture cannot change. Case: R-20.
