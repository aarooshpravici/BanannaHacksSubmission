**Changed in the rim fix of Oct 11 (version `39abe26`), after a tester's report (issue 6).** Get the update before you test: "Getting fixes" in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md#what-to-expect). The demo online has had it since Oct 11, 11:36 AM: if you had the demo open before then, reload the page.

- **A photo of your own no longer has a rim round every object.** A tester chose a photo of machines in front of open sky on the demo. Every object in it had a bright rim on the side towards the light and a dark rim on the other side, like a cut-out with a glow. That is fixed: the edge of an object is now lit like the flat area next to it.
- **What it changes:** a photo of your own that the browser analysed (the demo, Plan B, **Analyse in this browser**), and any photo with the **Detailed normals** switch off. The four samples did not change.
- **What it does not change:** on such a photo a face is still flatter than on a sample (K48). And the cast shadow still lands on open sky, as a soft dark band beside each object. On an outdoor photo that is plain to see. It is known and stays (K13): switch **Cast shadows** off, or raise **Height**.
- **Side effects, known (K72 to K75).** Near its edge an object is shaded as if it faced you: a round thing is flatter there, and a thin thing (a pole, an arm) is lit evenly right across, like card. A smooth, light, round thing can have a pale band along its edge on the side away from the light. A thin rim can stay on a very small photo, along a shallow step and beside whiskers. A faint second line can show just outside an object on a plain wall. And a photo of your own takes 0.1 to 0.3 seconds longer on the developer's Mac.
- **A rim round an object is now a FAIL**, apart from the thin ones of K73. Send a screenshot. No case is new; Q-12, P-12, P-04, Q-04, Q-08 and H-12 say what is expected now.
- **Hardly seen by a person yet.** The developer looked at the kit's four photos only. An independent check, again by an AI on the same Mac, then looked at about 20 real pictures and found the side effects above. One person has run the fix on a photo of their own: the tester of issue 6, at 12:27 PM, who sent two screenshots and no words about it. Nobody else has: item 2 below.

**Changed in the depth check update of Oct 11 (version `48b8c77`), after a tester's report (issue 4).** Get the update before you test: "Getting fixes" in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md#what-to-expect).

- **The page tests the graphics chip before it works out your own photo.** On one tester's Snapdragon laptop, both photos they tried came out covered in a regular grid of dimples, like hammered metal. The depth model had run on that graphics chip with no error and had given a wrong answer. Now, each time the page is loaded, it first runs the depth model on a small test picture of its own. If the graphics chip gets that picture wrong, the processor works out your photo instead: slower, and right.
- **Qualcomm graphics chips on Windows are not tried at all.** That is the kind of chip the fault was found on. The processor does the work there.
- **The depth line in GEOMETRY can say more.** Three wordings are new: "depth: this browser, processor (the graphics chip failed a check)", "…processor (the graphics chip failed)" and "…processor (this graphics chip is known to get the depth model wrong)".
- **The browser's console has a new line** that starts "Relight: the graphics chip". It holds your chip's scores in the test. We want it from every computer: case P-14.
- **Four options on the address, for testers:** `?depth=wasm`, `?depth=webgpu`, `?depth=check` and `?depth=auto` choose how the depth model is run (case P-16).
- Three cases are new: **P-14**, **P-15** and **P-16**. The four samples, the sun and the shading line did not change.
- **Confirmed since then on the Snapdragon laptop, in one browser (issues 6 and 4):** at the normal address the dimples are gone and the depth line reads "…processor (this graphics chip is known to get the depth model wrong)". With `?depth=check` the page's own test catches that chip without the list: agreement 0.6130 where 0.99 or more passes, roughness 185.52 where 6 or less passes. All four runs of P-15 came out as written. The browser was the Chromium 152 built into the Claude desktop app. **Still not known:** the same in Edge or Chrome. Nobody has run this on a phone, or on any graphics chip but that one and one Apple one.

## How to report

Use the two links under [Reporting what you found](README.md#reporting-what-you-found). One opens a filled-in form for a bug, the other for a finished test run. The developer reads those GitHub issues and nothing else, so anything said only in a chat will not reach them.

One bug per issue. Always include the case ID, your computer and browser, the photo (or the sample's name), a screenshot, and now also the **shading line**: both forms have a place for it. On the hosted demo, write "demo" where the form asks for the pill, and "hosted demo" with the page address where it asks for the version. Before you report, read "Already known: no need to report" at the top of [PENDING.md](PENDING.md#already-known-no-need-to-report): K72 to K75 were added in the rim fix of Oct 11, and K13, K14, K17 and K48 were reworded; K66 to K71 were added in the depth check update of Oct 11; K56 to K64 were added in the early morning update of Oct 11 and K39 was reworded; K48 to K55 were added in the late night update before it, and K19 was rewritten. Both forms ask for the shading line; the bug form now also asks where the photo's shape came from. Neither form has a place of its own for the console line of P-14 and P-15: paste it into the note of the case, whole.
