## How to report

Use the two links under [Reporting what you found](README.md#reporting-what-you-found). One opens a filled-in form for a bug, the other for a finished test run. The developer reads those GitHub issues and nothing else, so anything said only in a chat will not reach them.

One bug per issue. Always include the case ID, your computer and browser, the photo (or the sample's name), a screenshot, and now also the **shading line**: both forms have a place for it. On the hosted demo, write "demo" where the form asks for the pill, and "hosted demo" with the page address where it asks for the version. Before you report, read "Already known: no need to report" at the top of [PENDING.md](PENDING.md#already-known-no-need-to-report): K77 to K93 were added in the drafts update of Oct 11; K72 to K75 were added in the rim fix of Oct 11, and K13, K14, K17 and K48 were reworded; K66 to K71 were added in the depth check update of Oct 11; K56 to K64 were added in the early morning update of Oct 11 and K39 was reworded; K48 to K55 were added in the late night update before it, and K19 was rewritten. Both forms ask for the shading line; the bug form now also asks where the photo's shape came from. Neither form has a place of its own for the console line of P-14 and P-15: paste it into the note of the case, whole.
